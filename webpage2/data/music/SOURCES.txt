Songs on zitro.spot: where each melody and its notes come from.
Every melody is in the public domain: its composers died before 1926, or it is traditional and printed
before 1931. The notes are read from score A and compared note by note with score B, which is never
copied. Composer dates are confirmed by two independent sources.
