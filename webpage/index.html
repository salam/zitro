Built by
Matthias

Hi, I'm Matthias, the creator of Firebird. I believe AI should be accessible, portable, and delightful to use. Firebird started as an experiment to bring AI assistants into the physical world in the most elegant way possible.

As a developer and maker, I've always been fascinated by the intersection of hardware and software. Firebird is my answer to the question: "What if your AI assistant could fit in your pocket and work anywhere?"

I hope Firebird brings you as much joy and productivity as it has brought me in creating it.
