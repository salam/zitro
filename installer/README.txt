Install Zitro
=============

Puts Zitro on your M5StickC Plus2. Needs macOS 11 or newer. Nothing else to install.

HOW TO
------
1. Double-click "Install Zitro".
2. Choose your AI service and enter its key. Or click "Set up later".
3. Enter your WiFi. Or click "Set up later".
4. Plug the stick into your Mac with a USB-C data cable.
5. Click "Install Zitro" and keep the stick plugged in. It takes about a minute.

Set something up later? Join the WiFi called "zitro" with your phone or Mac and open
zitro.local. Triple-press the stick's side button to get there at any time.

ALREADY USING ZITRO?
--------------------
Your settings stay. Click "Set up later" twice and just update.

YOUR KEYS
---------
Your key and WiFi password go from this app straight to the stick over the USB cable.
They are not saved on your Mac and not sent anywhere else.

Used an installer before version 1.1? Those stored your key in the stick's memory in plain
text, and after a failed install could leave a copy in your Mac's temp folder. This version
wipes the copy on the stick, and a restart of your Mac clears the temp folder.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK
------------------
Stick not found:   Some USB-C cables only charge. Try another one, and turn the stick on.
Port in use:       Close Arduino and any serial monitor.
Install failed:    Click "Try again". Your stick is fine; it can always be installed again.
