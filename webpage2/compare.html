Every AI device trades something for something. Here's what each one really asks of you — attention, money, or privacy — and what it gives back.

Pocket stick · $25

Zitro

One button, one answer, then dark.

  • $25 once — no subscription, no account
  • Listens only while you press the button
  • No feed, notifications, or ads by design
  • Open-source firmware, your own AI key
  • Kid-safe calm: parental lock, nothing to scroll
  • Needs WiFi and your own AI key (OpenAI, Claude, or Infomaniak)
  • Small 1.14" screen — answers are read, not heard
  • Audio and camera modules still experimental

Best forKids' first AI, screen-time reducers, and anyone who wants answers without an ecosystem.

Pocket companion · price TBA

Meta Muse Charm

A Tamagotchi-style charm for Meta's Muse agent.

  • Tap the fingerprint sensor and talk — no phone, no app
  • Keychain-sized, with a tiny screen for its avatar
  • Reported 5G built in, so it works on its own
  • Not shipping yet — promised for December 2026, no price
  • A cartoon character designed to be checked on, like a pet
  • Your conversations live in a Meta account and Meta's cloud
  • Closed hardware from the company that runs the feeds

Best forMeta AI fans who want a cute always-available agent — once it ships.

Smart glasses · $299–799

Meta Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta, Galaxy Glasses

A camera and an assistant on your face.

  • Hands-free photos, video, and open-ear audio
  • Always with you once you wear them
  • Now from $299 (Meta Glasses); Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 from $449; Display with in-lens screen $799
  • Samsung's Gemini-powered Galaxy Glasses expected by end of 2026
  • A camera pointed at everyone you talk to
  • Wake word means the mic is always waiting
  • Tied to a Meta or Google account and cloud AI
  • Battery anxiety by lunchtime (6–9 h)

Best forContent creators who want hands-free capture — and are at peace with wearing a camera.

Pendants & wristbands · $75–249

Amazon Bee, Friend, Plaud

Wearables that remember your day.

  • Featherweight and screen-free (Bee Frontier is 8 g)
  • Ambient capture is great for notes and recall
  • Cheap entry: Bee Frontier $74.99, Plaud NotePin S $179
  • Bee and Friend record continuously — everyone around you is on the mic
  • Subscriptions for the good parts: Bee Pro $11.99/mo, Friend memory $10/mo, Plaud minutes
  • The big players are buying them up: Amazon bought Bee, Meta bought Limitless (and stopped selling it), Humane's Pin was bricked within a year
  • Useless without a phone app

Best forDedicated note-takers who accept always-on recording and a subscription.

AI handheld · $199

Rabbit R1

A playful orange pocket companion.

  • Dedicated device, distinctive design
  • Bigger screen and a scroll wheel
  • No subscription; rabbit OS3 lets you bring your own API key
  • Still updated — a rare survivor among 2024's AI gadgets
  • Queries processed in the vendor's cloud by default
  • Another app-like screen competing for attention
  • 115 g — seven times heavier than Zitro
  • Usefulness beyond a phone is debated

Best forGadget enthusiasts who want to experiment with an AI-native device.

Already in your pocket

Your phone + a chat app

The most capable AI you own.

  • Most powerful models, voice modes, vision
  • No new hardware to buy
  • Always up to date
  • Every question opens the attention casino — feed, badges, "one quick look"
  • Accounts, history, and data tied to a vendor
  • Not a device you hand a seven-year-old

Best forDeep work sessions at a desk — when you trust yourself not to wander.

On the shelf · $50+

Smart speaker

Alexa+, Google Home & co. — and OpenAI's rumored puck.

  • Hands-free for the whole household
  • Great for timers, music, smart home
  • Alexa+ is now included with Prime; OpenAI's Jony Ive speaker is reported for 2027 at $200–400
  • A microphone that's always waiting in the room
  • Tied to a shopping/advertising ecosystem
  • Answers are spoken over everyone present
  • Stays home — no help on a walk

Best forKitchen timers and music — less so for curious questions on the move.
