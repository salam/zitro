Songs on zitro.spot: where each melody and its notes come from.
Every melody is in the public domain: its composers died before 1926, or it is traditional and printed
before 1931. The notes are read from score A and compared note by note with score B, which is never
copied. Composer dates are confirmed by two independent sources.

auld-lang-syne
  Names: Auld Lang Syne / Auld Lang Syne
  Score A (the notes): Auld Lang Syne, arr. Sir Herbert Oakeley, in The Scottish Students' Song Book, S. 318-319, Bayley & Ferguson, London 1897 | https://archive.org/details/scottishstudents00scot_0/page/n325/ | pre-1931 print
  Score B (compared only): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auld_Lang_Syne
  Traditional, printed in: Traditional Scots tune, first documented with Burns's words in George Thomson's Select Collection of Original Scottish Airs, Edinburgh 1799 (1799) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auld_Lang_Syne
  Traditional, printed in: The Scottish Students' Song Book, Bayley & Ferguson, London 1897, S. 318 (1897) | https://archive.org/details/scottishstudents00scot_0/page/n325/
  Settled: Matthias 2026-11-07: no open score of the 2/4 version exists, so B (en.wikipedia, moved +2) is 4/4 at doubled values and also differs in bar 9 (A D5:2 B4:2 B4:2 G4:2, B halved D5:3 B4:1 B4:2 G4:2) and bar 10 (A A4:3 G4:1 A4:2 B4:2, B halved A4:3 G4:1 A4:2 E5:2); Zitro plays the old print's version (1897), and a second reading of A's scan confirmed A.
