SONG LIBRARY: APPROVALS AND RULINGS
===================================

This file records every decision behind the server songs in scripts/music_library/,
so that the library can be rebuilt and checked the same way later.

Three kinds of decision are recorded:

  A. Approvals by Matthias, the project owner. Only he may approve a difference
     between score A and score B, and every such approval is also written into the song
     file as a "settled Matthias <date>: ..." line. The tool refuses any other difference.
  B. Rulings made by the AI controller while building the library (2026-11-07). Each
     gives the decision, the reason, and the cost if the ruling is wrong. Each one may
     be overruled.
  C. Where the evidence lives.

The design rules themselves are in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-song-library-design.md.
The design marks decisions taken from Matthias with "(user)".


A. APPROVALS BY MATTHIAS
------------------------

2026-11-07  General rules (design discussion)
  - "Ok, wenn es Lieder nicht findet. Hohe confidence ist besser."
    A missing song is fine. A wrong note or a legal doubt is not.
  - "Sicher global ungeschützt": all authors died before 1926, or the melody is
    traditional with a print before 1931, and the melody was first published before 1931.
  - The evidence must come from two independent reputable sources that agree. A
    traditional melody also needs a named print from before 1931.

2026-11-07  Rest folding (question: does a note followed by a rest count as a longer note?)
  Answer: "Als gleich werten (Empfohlen)"
  Rule: a note followed by a rest counts as equal to a longer note of the same pitch,
  when the next note starts at the same time. The tool implements this, and also
  applies it at the end of a song, where the song end counts as the next note (ruling B27).

C. WHERE THE EVIDENCE LIVES
---------------------------
  scripts/music_library/<id>.song   score A notes, the score A source, score B (URL, licence,
                                    notes), the legal evidence, the settled line
  webpage2/data/music/SOURCES.txt   the published evidence per song (built by the tool)
  evidence/transcripts-*.txt        score A readings, logged before any other score of
                                    the song was opened (this keeps them independent)
  evidence/research-*.md            research notes per round: the sources searched, the
                                    differences between A and B, crop positions on A's
                                    scan, and the songs dropped and why
                                    (round1 = the first five songs; de, en, ever = round 2)

The scan crops mentioned in the notes (for example "crop x900-1560 y1680-1960") were
temporary files and are not kept. They can be made again from the archive.org page
image named next to each crop, at the stated pixel size. "<scratchpad>" in the notes
stands for that temporary folder.

The notes are working records written during the research. Where they disagree with a
.song file, the .song file is the one that counts. In particular, the notes still list
long-long-ago and humpty-dumpty, which were dropped later (rulings B38, B40), and their
"final tool output" sections show the state at that time.
